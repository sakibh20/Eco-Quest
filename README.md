# 🌱 Eco-Quest 🌍  
*A Planetary Survival Game Rooted in Ecological Awareness*

## 🧠 Concept  
**Eco-Quest** is a hybrid educational-exploration game where the player becomes the first settler on a newly habitable planet. The fate of the ecosystem is shaped entirely by the player's decisions.

This project is currently in the **concept and UI prototyping phase**, with a fully developed game ideation pitch and partially working interface built in Unity.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview
- **Scan & Learn:** Players explore biomes and scan flora and fauna to gather knowledge and earn XP.
- **Ripple Impact System:** Every action—planting trees, removing species, overharvesting—affects the long-term evolution of the planet.
- **Visual Feedback:** The environment evolves based on player behavior. Players witness thriving nature or ecological collapse.
- **Goal:** Build a sustainable ecosystem that supports future human civilization.

---

## 🧪 Educational Goals
- Promote ecological empathy and awareness.
- Demonstrate how individual actions have cumulative effects.
- Connect players to biodiversity through interactive scanning and facts.
